1. INTRODUCTION

MediScan AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application MediScan AI
(the "App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use
our service.

By using MediScan AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

a) Medical Report Files
   - Images (JPG, PNG) of medical reports you upload for analysis.
   - PDF files of medical reports you upload for analysis.
   - These files are sent directly to OpenAI's API for AI-powered analysis.
   - We do NOT store these files on any of our servers.

b) Analysis Results
   - The AI-generated analysis of your medical reports is stored
     locally on your device only.
   - No analysis data is transmitted to or stored on our servers.

c) OpenAI API Key
   - If you provide your own OpenAI API key in the Settings screen,
     it is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage.
   - Your API key is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

d) Device Information
   - We do not collect any device identifiers, location data,
     or personal information.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

The medical report files you upload are used solely for the purpose of:
   - Sending to OpenAI's API (https://openai.com) to generate an
     AI-powered analysis of your report.
   - Displaying the analysis results to you within the App.

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or any other purpose.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES — OPENAI

MediScan AI uses OpenAI's API to analyze your medical reports.
When you submit a report for analysis:
   - The content of your report (image or text) is sent to OpenAI.
   - OpenAI processes this data according to their own Privacy Policy,
     available at: https://openai.com/privacy

We strongly recommend:
   - Removing or blurring personal identifying information (name,
     ID number, address) from reports before uploading.
   - Reviewing OpenAI's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

We are not responsible for how OpenAI handles data transmitted to
their API.

5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

   - All analysis history is stored locally on your device only.
   - No data is stored on our servers or in the cloud.
   - You can delete all stored analysis history at any time from
     the History tab within the App.
   - Your OpenAI API key is stored using your device's secure
     local storage and is never shared with anyone.

MediScan AI is NOT a medical device and does NOT provide medical advice,
diagnosis, or treatment.

   - The AI-generated analysis is for informational purposes only.
